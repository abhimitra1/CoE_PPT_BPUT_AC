04  /  PATHS CoE

Physical → Digital → Physical

A craft object carries knowledge that was never written down

  • Photograph a craft piece; AI reconstructs it as a 3D digital twin.
  • Its maker, a student or a researcher adds the story, material and technique behind it.
  • The design is explored, redesigned with AI, or downloaded — every result keeps a visible link back to what it was based on.
  • The improved design is remade as a real object, by hand.
The commitment

Throwing, glazing and firing stay wholly human. Computation handles only what comes before the making act — and what verifies it afterwards.

04  /  PATHS CoE

Live at paths.cutm.ac.in

Working end to end, from photograph to fired piece

1

Capture & Digitise

  • Photograph a piece; AI returns a 3D digital twin.
  • Maker, student or researcher records story, material and technique.
  • Each twin becomes a searchable entry in a living archive.
2

Reimagine & Publish

  • AI-assisted redesign that starts from tradition, with visible lineage.
  • Twins freeze at real-world scale and publish; review and ratification run end to end.
  • View any twin in AR at true scale, on your own device.
3

Make & Grow

  • The design returns to the artisan's hands — throwing, glazing, firing stay human.
  • The fired piece is checked back against its twin, closing the loop.
  • Pottery first; bamboo, textiles, wood and metal follow the same loop.
Why this matters to BPUT

Odisha's craft clusters are a live economy, not a heritage exhibit. PATHS turns them into project work, publishable research and student IP — and a twelve-month cluster study is designed and ready to run. The same platform can carry a project bank for any affiliated college with a craft cluster nearby.

What we propose

The model, run deliberately

Build · Operate · Scale · Transfer

VEDIC has already proved the model inside a BPUT college. We propose running it deliberately, and repeating it.
